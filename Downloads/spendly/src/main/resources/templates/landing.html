The Spendly principle

We think money should never be a mystery. Every rupee has a job. A budget is a plan, not a punishment. A bill paid on time is a perfectly calm morning, and the best months are the ones you saw coming. So we built a planner for knowing exactly, quietly, where your money goes.

Income against expenses, budgets by category, bills and subscriptions, savings goals and a month-end forecast. Built in, not bolted on.

Do transfers count as spending?

No. Moving money between your own accounts, including paying a credit-card bill, changes balances but never your income or expenses.

How does the month-end forecast work?

It adds the bills and income still due from your recurring items to your day-to-day spending pace, blended with the last three months early in the month.

What does safe to spend mean?

This month's income, minus what you have spent, the bills still due and your savings target. Divided by the days left, it is a daily allowance.

Can I set budgets quickly?

Yes. Copy last month's budgets in one click, or let Spendly suggest each category's limit from your last three months of spending.

Can I use a currency other than rupees?

Yes. Pick a currency when you sign up or change it under My account. Rupees use Indian grouping, like 1,25,000.

Who can see my money?

Only you. Every account sees only its own books, and passwords are stored as one-way hashes.
